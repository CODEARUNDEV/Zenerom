FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can we see measurable results from SEO vs Google Ads?

Google Ads and performance marketing generate qualified inbound inquiries within 48 to 72 hours of campaign launch. SEO is a compounding long-term growth asset where noticeable ranking improvements and Google Map Pack traction emerge within 60 to 90 days, followed by sustained, cost-effective organic dominance.

Do we get a dedicated account manager and team based in Dubai?

Yes. Every client is assigned a dedicated growth manager based at our Dubai office in Burjuman Business Tower. You have direct communication via phone, email, and dedicated WhatsApp groups, alongside weekly performance check-ins and monthly transparent reports.

How does your dedicated developer and IT outsourcing model work?

Our IT outsourcing allows you to hire full-time developers, web specialists, and SEO experts dedicated 100% to your company under strict SLAs. We manage technical screening, equipment, and administrative overhead, relieving you of UAE visa costs, health insurance, and end-of-service gratuity liabilities.

Can Zenerom implement or migrate our CRM without sales downtime?

Absolutely. Our certified Zoho CRM engineers build, test, and validate data migration in a sandbox staging environment. Once verified, we execute seamless live cutovers with zero operational disruption, followed by comprehensive staff training and pipeline optimization.

What contracts and engagement models are available?

We offer both flexible monthly retainers for continuous marketing and SEO, and milestone-based fixed contracts for web and application engineering. We believe in earning your business every single month through transparent, measurable ROI rather than restrictive multi-year lock-ins.

Do you offer bilingual English and Arabic campaigns?

Yes. All our digital marketing, SEO, Google Ads copy, and website landing pages are natively crafted and localized in both English and professional Arabic to maximize reach and trust across local Emirati, GCC, and expat demographics.
